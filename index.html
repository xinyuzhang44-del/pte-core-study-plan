Week 1 · 口语冲刺 Speaking Push — 50%Aug 10–16
  • Read Aloud 每天30-40题：不停顿、不重读、不自我纠正，生词跳过或蒙音，流利比准确重要。
    30-40 items/day — never pause, never re-read, never self-correct; skip unknown words. Fluency beats accuracy.
  • Repeat Sentence 每天30-50句：抓主谓宾，自己的话流利说出来，重复两遍也行。
    30-50 sentences/day — catch subject-verb-object, say it fluently, repeating twice is fine.
  • Describe Image / Re-tell Lecture：第一天背熟一个模板，之后每天5-8题保持手感。
    Memorize one template on day 1, then 5-8 items/day just to stay sharp.
Week 2 · 阅读突破 Reading Push — 50%Aug 17–23
  • FIB 填空是核心：高频题库刷一遍，生词/搭配记本子上每天看，高频背熟，冷门不管。
    Fill in the Blanks is the core lever — go through the bank once, log new words/collocations, review daily.

    Re-order Paragraphs 10-15/day — spot connectors and pronouns, cap 1 min/item, guess if stuck.

    Summarize Spoken Text — template it, catch keywords, slot them in.

  1. 口语最容易快速提分——评分看流利度，不是发音语法完美。
    Speaking is your fastest win — scored on fluency, not perfection.
  2. WFD 性价比最高——一份时间，听力写作两份回报。
    WFD is best ROI — one effort, two skill scores.
  3. Writing 别多花时间——已经74了，模板+校对就够。
    Don't over-invest in Writing — it's already close.
  4. Reading 没有捷径——需要持续积累，尽早开始别拖到最后。
    Reading has no shortcut — start early, needs sustained time.
  5. 模考分数可能偏乐观——多平台交叉验证，别只信一个。
    Mock scores can run optimistic — cross-check across platforms.

各题型速查卡 Task-Type Cheat Sheets

上面的五周计划已经够用了——这里是想深入了解某个题型时再看。
The 5-week plan above is enough on its own — come back here only if you want more detail on a specific task.

Speaking Read Aloud · Tier 1

  • Fluency beats pronunciation perfection — a steady, moderate pace scores higher than a hesitant "correct" one. A metronome app helps train the rhythm.
  • Don't know a word? Skip it or sound it out by spelling rules — never stall.
  • Stumble? Keep going — never restart or re-read, that costs more than the slip.

Speaking Repeat Sentence · Tier 1

  • Memorize by meaning, not word-by-word — chunk into phrases. Working memory holds ~7 items; grouping beats brute recall.
  • Fluency beats completeness — catch subject-verb-object and say it confidently even with gaps in the middle.
  • Only caught half the sentence? Say what you have — twice, fluently — rather than trailing off once.

Speaking Describe Image · Tier 1

  • The most template-able task in the test — 3-part structure: overview → key trend/data → brief conclusion.
  • Fill the ~35–40s window; silence costs fluency points.
  • Full template variants below in Full Templates.

Speaking Re-tell Lecture / ASQ / Group Discussion · Tier 2

  • Re-tell Lecture: jot 3–4 keywords while listening, structure topic → points → conclusion.
  • Answer Short Question: answer in 1–3 words instantly, don't explain — wrong guesses aren't penalized, so never leave one blank.
  • Group Discussion: track each speaker's stance, summarize the overall arc. Full templates below in Full Templates.

Writing Summarize Written Text

  • One sentence, 25–50 words (Core spec) — use subordination (although/which/because), not two sentences.
  • Capture the main idea plus one supporting point.
  • Check grammar and the single closing period before submitting.

Writing Write Essay

  • 200–300 words, 20 minutes: intro (paraphrase + stance) → 2–3 body paragraphs → conclusion.
  • Adapt any template to the actual prompt — generic, unmodified templates are scored down under current rules.
  • One idea + one example per body paragraph, not a list of unsupported claims.

Reading Re-order Paragraphs

  • Find the topic sentence first — the one with no backward reference. Budget ~90 seconds.
  • Chain the rest by pronouns (it/they/this) and connectors referencing prior sentences.
  • Re-read the assembled order once before moving on.

Reading Fill in the Blanks / MCQ

  • Use collocations and grammar (part of speech, subject-verb agreement) to eliminate options fast.
  • Bottom-up: if the first blank is unclear, read on for context before committing.
  • Multiple-answer MCQ: don't over-select — wrong picks are penalized.

Listening Write From Dictation

  • Plays once — jot fragments immediately, reconstruct the grammar after.
  • Partial credit per word: always write something, never leave it blank.

Listening Summarize Spoken Text

  • Short-form notes for key points, examples, and topic shifts while listening.
  • 20–30 words for Core (not Academic's 50–70) — going over costs Form points.
  • Neutral tone, no personal opinion, stick to the speaker's core takeaway.

Listening HCS / SMW / HIW / FIB

  • Predict word type or missing content from context before the audio starts.
  • Highlight Incorrect Words: listen for words that don't fit the sentence's meaning, not just odd sounds.
  • Highlight Correct Summary: eliminate summaries with claims the audio didn't support.

Re-tell Lecture — full template

~40s response, from notes — never read back verbatim.

标准三段式
This lecture is mainly about 话题. The speaker points out that 关键点1, and also mentions 关键点2. For example, 关键描述/举例. In conclusion, the lecture highlights the importance of 话题/结论.

多要点递进

提示：用关键词记笔记，不写全句；40秒内说满3-4句，宁可简洁完整，不要卡壳。

Answer Short Question — pattern bank

1–3 words only, ~9–10s window, wrong answers aren't penalized further — never leave one blank.

  • 定义命名类 "What do we call...?" → Dentist / Iron / Chemist
  • 反义词类 "Opposite of...?" → Natural / Antagonist / Cold
  • 近义词类 "Another word for...?" → Glad / Large
  • 数量事实类 "How many...?" → Seven / Three / Eleven
  • 分类归属类 "Name a type of...?" → Banana / Puppy / Pride
  • 常识科学类 → Photosynthesis / Mercury / Violin

口诀：听关键词 → 想类别 → 只说词，不说句 → 立刻开口，宁猜不空。

Summarize Group Discussion — full template

10s prep + 2min response, dual-scored into Listening + Speaking. Content coverage of every speaker matters most.

精简单句版（保险省时）
The discussion focused on 话题, where 说话人A argued that 观点A because 理由A, while 说话人B disagreed, contending that 观点B; 说话人C took a middle ground, suggesting 观点C, and the group ultimately agreed that / remained divided over 结论.

表达库：X argued that... / Y disagreed, pointing out that... / Z raised a further point, noting... / In the end, the group agreed that... / remained divided on..., with no clear resolution.
